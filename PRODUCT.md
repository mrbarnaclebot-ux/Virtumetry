# Product

## Register

product

## Users

v1: one owner on one iPhone 17 Pro, testing the loop end to end. Later: real-estate brokers who scan properties, and buyers or renters who tour them in a browser. Context of use: standing in a real room holding the phone at chest height and walking slowly while scanning (mixed indoor light, one hand busy, attention on the room, not the screen); later, reviewing and sharing the finished room from the couch or a desk. Share-link visitors open the room in any browser with no account.

## Product Purpose

Turn a slow walk-through of a real room into a photoreal, real-scale 3D world that can be walked, measured and shared on the phone and in any browser. Every Echo-3 run costs real money (about $15–18), so the app must make bad captures obvious before upload, show cost before spending, and report processing states honestly. Success: the scan comes back as a faithful, walkable world, reliably, without wasting paid processing.

## Brand Personality

Exact, calm, spatial. Like a well-made surveying instrument: quiet confidence, precise readouts, nothing decorative that doesn't measure or guide. Voice is short, plain and honest about cost, time and failure; it never oversells. The emotional goal is trust ("this tool knows what it is doing with my room and my money") with a small moment of wonder when the room first appears in 3D.

## Anti-references

- SpAItial's own app (look, copy and flow must not be copied).
- Generic "AI app" dark mode: neon gradients, glowing orbs, glass cards everywhere, gradient text.
- SaaS dashboard tropes: hero metrics, identical card grids, side-stripe callouts.
- Real-estate portal clutter (badges, banners, upsells).
- Toy-like AR apps with bouncy, elastic motion.

## Design Principles

1. **Honest states over optimistic ones.** Every loading, empty, error, offline and cost state says what is true and what to do next. No placeholder or dead control ships.
2. **Guide the hands, not just the eyes.** During capture the user looks at the room; guidance is glanceable, haptic and short.
3. **The room is the hero.** Chrome recedes around camera feeds and 3D worlds; colour and motion are spent on state and guidance.
4. **Money is a first-class state.** Cost, budget and retry are always explicit and confirmed, never implied.
5. **Earned familiarity.** Standard iOS patterns and affordances, refined; personality lives in type, colour and a few crafted moments (onboarding, first world reveal).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text and UI components. 44pt minimum touch targets. VoiceOver labels on every control. Dynamic Type respected with caps where layout would break. Reduce Motion honoured everywhere (fade or instant). Status never conveyed by colour alone (icon + text).
