## What was built

- `ProgressBar({ value: number|null, height?: 4|6, tone?: 'accent'|'success', accessibilityLabel, testID, style })`: `accessibilityRole="progressbar"`, `accessibilityValue` with `now` only when determinate, `accessibilityState.busy` when indeterminate. The fill has testID `${testID}-fill`. Under Reduce Motion the width jumps and the segment stays at the left.
- `Skeleton({ width, height, radius? })`: `accessible={false}`, opacity 0.5 to 1 over 1200 ms linear, reversing. Static at 0.7 under Reduce Motion. `SkeletonRow` is a 64x64 thumb plus bars at 70% and 40%, min height 96. The caller's container carries the a11y label and busy state.
- `Banner`: warning, danger, info and neutral tones on a fully tinted surface with no side stripe. The 20 px icon is in the tone colour. Warning and danger get the alert role. It takes an optional dismiss `IconButton` (TS-typed: `dismissLabel` is required when `onDismiss` is set) and an optional action. It fades in over 200 ms and out over 150 ms with a `LinearTransition` on the wrapper.
- `EmptyState`: art panel (radius xl), heading with header role, body, eyebrow numeral steps, and a primary lg CTA 48 below. The compact variant shows icon (24), bodyStrong title and body, and no art.
- `ErrorState`: 48 px icon (muted or danger), heading with header role, muted body, secondary button, `cap` for maxFontSizeMultiplier, and a `children` slot for extra actions.
