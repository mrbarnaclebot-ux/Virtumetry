## Top Priority Fixes

1. **WARNING (major): the money claim contradicts itself.** `src/library/display-state.ts:201-208` puts the line "Not charged" on local `failed` rows. `src/app/capture/[id].tsx:300-301` says on purpose that "its charge isn't known on the phone, so no charge claim here". The user reads "Not charged" in the Library, then opens the scan and sees no such promise. The project's core constraint is honesty about $15–18 runs.
   - **Fix:** use `withDate('Not charged', date)` only for `blocked`. For `failed`, drop the charge claim (`withDate(null, date)`), or say "Charge unknown", which `display-state.ts:148` already uses.
2. **WARNING (major): on the capture HUD, the normal mid-scan state shows a red X.** `src/components/readiness-pill.tsx:37` tints the icon with `tint` (danger) even when `scheme="dark"`. Only the label switches to `hudTint` (text). The comment at line 13 says "Keep scanning is the normal mid-scan state, not an alarm". So every scan starts with a red `xmark.circle.fill` next to a white headline. That breaks round 1 P0 (readiness as a calm headline) and adds a mixed signal.
   - **Fix:** `color={hud ? hudTint : tint}` on the Icon. For red-on-HUD, either use a neutral glyph or drop the icon.
3. **WARNING (major): a failed share fails silently.** In `src/app/world/[id]/share.tsx:148`, `shareUrl(s.url).catch(() => {})` swallows every failure. If the share sheet can't open, nothing happens. This is exactly the "zombie control" UX-07 bans.
   - **Fix:** `.catch(() => toast.error("Couldn't open sharing. Try again."))`. A user cancel resolves rather than rejects, so no false toasts.
4. **WARNING: onboarding copy can clip.** In `src/components/onboarding/pager.tsx:37,75-82`, the art is a fixed 52% of window height. The `display` headline (capped at 44 pt) and a 3-line `body` (capped at 1.6×, which wraps to 5 lines or more) sit in a non-scrolling page above the pinned dots and button. On a 667-pt device at large Dynamic Type, the body is cut off. That is a UX-08 failure.
   - **Fix:** wrap each page's copy in a vertical `ScrollView`, or clamp `artH` to `Math.min(window.height * 0.52, window.height - <copy+controls estimate>)`. Shrinking the art as the font scale grows also works.
5. **WARNING: the hero-metric pattern survived round 2.** `src/components/approval.tsx:145-156` stacks four money lines: a 28-pt `~$9.30`, a mono `930 credits`, then `~$40.70 left this month` and a caption `4,070 credits`. Round 2 named this as an AI-slop tell, but no fix row addressed it, and fix #7 added another line.
   - **Fix:** collapse it to the cost readout, then one muted line "930 credits · ~$40.70 left this month", and drop the separate budget-credits caption.
6. **WARNING: a mono eyebrow is still on a non-readout.** In `src/app/capture/scan.tsx:285`, "Test scenario" uses `type="eyebrow"` (Martian Mono, uppercase). The addendum says "Mono eyebrows are reserved for readouts", and round 2 #8 is marked as fixed. This is the only remaining `eyebrow` use in the app.
   - **Fix:** `type="label" tone="muted"`.
7. **WARNING: in Review, the destructive action is styled inconsistently.** At `src/app/capture/[id].tsx:141`, the failed-checks branch renders Discard as a filled `secondary` (indigo) button. The passing branch, line 146, uses `variant="destructive"` (ghost danger text). A destructive action gets more visual weight exactly when the user is most likely to tap it by reflex.
   - **Fix:** `variant="destructive"` in both branches.
8. **WARNING: an accent fill on the indigo field has no ring.** At `src/components/library-empty-art.tsx:27`, `<Circle {...dot} fill={color.accent} />` sits on the `secondary` field (about 1.8:1). Round 2 #9 set the rule that accent fills on the field keep a text-coloured ring, and the onboarding art follows it (`art.tsx:79,154`). The empty-state art missed it, so the brand-mark vertex nearly disappears.
   - **Fix:** add `stroke={color.text} strokeWidth={0.4}` (in 24-grid units) to match.

## Detailed Findings

### Pillar 1: Copywriting (3/4)

**What passes:**
- The em-dash, en-dash and `--` grep over app/components/viewer/library/capture returns no user-facing hits.
- Toasts are short, verb or object first, with no exclamation marks. All 13 `toast.*` call sites were checked.
- The destructive confirmations match §6 and the addendum: "Turn off link" / "Keep link", and the delete body "our servers, including processed files" (round 2 #13). Rename now says "Save" (round 2 #12).
- The Review primary is "Check cost" and the approve labels are "Approve and process" / "Approve and retry". The upload titles are honest: Uploaded, Upload paused, Upload failed, "Didn't pass checks".

**Findings:**
- **WARNING:** `src/library/display-state.ts:207-208`. A local `failed` upload says "Not charged", which contradicts `src/app/capture/[id].tsx:300-301`. See Fix 1.
- **WARNING:** the viewer copy is touch-only on the desktop web share page. These strings don't use the `t(touch, desktop)` helper, although the mode hints at lines 385-410 do:
  - `src/viewer/world-viewer.tsx:615` "Tap to jump there"
  - `:706` "Tap two points"
  - `:591` aria "Floor plan. Tap a spot to move there."
- **Minor:** the room/scan noun drifts on the world screen. `src/app/world/[id]/index.tsx` shows "Opening room" and "Share this room" next to "Couldn't open this scan", "This scan isn't available" and "This scan isn't ready yet". Pick "room" once the scan is on the server, which matches the viewer's "Couldn't load this room".
- **Minor:** `src/app/capture/prep.tsx:148` uses the generic label "Continue". The checker sign-off asked for verb+noun ("Continue to scan").
- **Minor:** in `src/app/world/[id]/share.tsx:84-90`, the create-link failure helper shows as `label` danger text without the `alert` icon that §5 TextField errors use, when the password switch is off.

### Pillar 2: Visuals (3/4)

**What passes:**
- Each main screen has a clear focal point: the Library title with one primary, the HUD readiness headline as `hudStatus` 22/28, the Review 4:3 hero frame with a 48-pt filmstrip, and the quote readout.
- Icon-only controls are labelled: `IconButton` requires `accessibilityLabel`, and the row More, banner dismiss and Share buttons were checked.
- The Library uses full-bleed rows with 4:3 88×66 thumbnails. The local first frame and the dated titles landed.

**Findings:**
- **WARNING:** `src/components/approval.tsx:145-156`. The hero-metric money stack is still there. See Fix 5.
- **WARNING:** the component system is bypassed by four hand-rolled buttons. They reimplement `Button` with no press scale (§11 asks for 0.97 on all Button/IconButton presses) and, in some cases, no focus ring:
  - `src/app/world/[id]/share.tsx:150-158` ("Turn off", no focusRing)
  - `src/components/rename-modal.tsx:68-80` ("Save")
  - `src/app/capture/scan.tsx:254-260` (HUD "Cancel")
  - `src/components/ui/banner.tsx:52-64` (link action)

  The root cause is that `Button` can't take a separate `accessibilityLabel` and has no primary-ink ghost variant. Add `accessibilityLabel?` and a `variant="link"` to `Button` once, then delete the four copies.
- **WARNING:** in `src/components/library-row.tsx:155-156`, `ReadyPulse` draws its own `Icon name="ready"` absolutely over the `StatusPill` icon, which is still rendered underneath. While the pulse scales to 1.25 there are two overlapping checkmarks, and the static one shows around the edges.
  - **Fix:** let `StatusPill` accept an animated icon style, or hide its icon while `justReady` is set.
- **Minor:** in `src/components/readiness-pill.tsx:37`, the red X icon sits under a white headline on the HUD (Fix 2). This is a visual mixed signal as well as a UX one.
- **Minor:** in `src/app/world/[id]/index.tsx:286`, `titlePad: { marginRight: 44 }` claims to "balance Back". The Back button is chevron plus "Back" (about 90 pt), and the title is left-aligned, so the padding balances nothing. Remove it or match the measured width.
- **Minor (brand):** the rendered `assets/images/icon.png` is a generic isometric wireframe cube with a heavy Y. It is close to SF Symbol `cube.transparent`, which the Library also uses as its world placeholder (`library-row.tsx:147`), so the brand mark and a system glyph read alike. It no longer reads as a letter, but it isn't distinctive. Decide this in Phase 7, before the dev-build icon ships.

### Pillar 3: Color (3/4)

**What passes:**
- The hex/rgba grep outside `src/design/tokens.ts`, tests and fixtures returns **0 hits**.
- `color.accent` appears only on these surfaces:
  - ProgressBar fills (`progress-bar.tsx:59`, `[id].tsx:397`, viewer `:866-867`)
  - CoachMark borders (`coach-mark.tsx:139,151`, viewer `:982,998-999`)
  - onboarding art fills with text rings
  - the active onboarding dot

  That matches §1.1's reserved list.
- `color.primary` is used in 17 files. The uses are primary Button fills, the Switch on-track, RefreshControl tint, the focus ring, the coverage arcs, the selected segment, the selected filmstrip thumb, the BrandMark vertex and the rename "Save" ink. All are on the reserved list or allowed by §5.
- Status colours are always paired with an icon and a word (StatusPill, Banner, ReadinessPill, quote over-budget line).

**Findings:**
- **WARNING:** in `src/components/library-empty-art.tsx:27`, the accent dot sits on the `secondary` field with no text ring (about 1.8:1). See Fix 8.
- **Minor:** `src/components/ui/banner.tsx:78`. The banner action link (`Refresh`) is mint `primary`. Text links aren't on §1.1's primary list, and on the Library this puts a second mint call to action next to the header's "New scan" primary. Use `text` with an underline or `info`, or accept it and add "banner action link" to the reserved list.
- **Minor:** `src/components/readiness-pill.tsx:11-14`. On the HUD, the danger tint is used for a non-error state (Fix 2).

There are no raw `fontSize` values in app/components except the BrandMark wordmark (`brand-mark.tsx:37`, size×0.7, which is acceptable for a logo lockup). The viewer derives every size from `type.*`.

**Findings:**
- **WARNING:** `src/app/capture/scan.tsx:285`. The last non-readout mono eyebrow is "Test scenario" (Fix 6). Round 2 #8 is marked done, but this one was missed.
- **Minor:** two off-role weights:
  - `src/components/approval.tsx:106,200` renders `caption` at `fontWeight: '600'` for the quote lead-in.
  - `src/components/ui/banner.tsx:243,76` renders `label` at 600 for banner titles.

  Neither caption/600 nor label/600 is a §1.3 role. Use `bodyStrong` for the banner title, and plain `caption` muted or `label` for the quote lead-in.
- **Minor:** `src/components/ui/segmented-control.tsx:84` and the viewer at `:1054,1069` use label/600 for the selected segment and the plan title. This one is allowed by §5 ("selected = label/600"), so it is noted only.

### Pillar 5: Spacing (3/4)

**What passes:**
- A grep for raw numeric margin/padding/gap/position in app/components returns only two hits:
  - `(tabs)/index.tsx:328` separator inset 104, which is the 88 thumb plus the 16 gap, derived and commented
  - `world/[id]/index.tsx:286` `marginRight: 44`, a Pillar 2 finding

  Everything else uses `space.*`, `radius.*`, `minTarget` or `layout.*`.
- Targets: Buttons are 52/44, IconButton is 44, list rows are at least 52, the HUD Cancel has `minTarget`, and the filmstrip is 48 + `hitSlop` 4.

**Findings:**
- **WARNING:** `src/components/onboarding/pager.tsx:37,75-82`. The page has no vertical scroll, the art height is fixed, and the copy has no room for Dynamic Type (Fix 4).
- **Minor:** pinned action areas are inconsistent.
  - `src/components/approval.tsx:191-197` (`Layout`, used by review, quote, upload and retry) has a hairline top, 16 padding and `bg`.
  - `src/app/capture/prep.tsx:179` (`pinned: { paddingTop: 12, paddingBottom: 16 }`) has no hairline and 12 top padding.

  §7 says pinned action areas "keep the existing Layout". Prep should reuse `Layout` or the same `styles.actions`.
- **Minor:** in `src/app/capture/scan.tsx:234`, the coach-mark anchor fallback uses the magic number `76` for the scenario block height. If the eyebrow role changes (Fix 6), the coach caret is misplaced until `measureInWindow` reports. Derive the number or measure the scenario block.
- **Minor:** in `src/components/library-row.tsx:182-183`, the pressed background applies only to the inner `Pressable` (`main`) and not to the More button column. A pressed row shows a highlight that stops 44 pt short of the trailing edge.

### Pillar 6: Experience Design (3/4)

**State coverage verified:**

| Screen | States |
|---|---|
| Library | 4 skeleton rows (`library-loading`), offline with no cache, error with no cache plus Try again, first-run empty with a conditional steps list, offline and refresh-failed banners, a budget banner with per-session dismiss, an honest delete-error toast (`lib/api.ts:141-143`), and a More button hidden when no actions exist (round 2 #1) |
| Quote | skeleton, offline, error plus Try again, over-budget with a disabled-reason hint |
| Upload | determinate, indeterminate and paused progress; blocked and failed states, each with a next step (round 2 #2, #4) |
| World | loading, 404, error plus Try again, not-ready plus Back to Library, capped plus Reload, and Reconnecting |
| Share | skeleton, error plus Try again, empty, offline banner with Create disabled plus a hint |
| Web | checking, not found, error, gate, crashed |

A capture start failure triggers a toast plus a haptic (round 2 #6).

Every destructive action has an `Alert` confirmation: discard (twice), delete, delete photos, cancel scan, log out, turn off link.

**Findings:**
- **WARNING:** `src/app/world/[id]/share.tsx:148`. Share-link failures are silent (Fix 3).
- **WARNING:** `src/components/readiness-pill.tsx:37`. The HUD shows an alarm glyph in the normal state (Fix 2).
- **WARNING:** `src/components/onboarding/pager.tsx`. Copy clips under Dynamic Type on small screens (Fix 4).
- **Minor:** `src/app/world/[id]/retry.tsx:146-170`. After a `not_retryable` error, `closed` hides Approve but the `QuoteCard` still shows a price that can no longer be approved, and there is no "Scan again" primary, unlike `NotRetryable`. Render `<NotRetryable>` (or its actions) when `closed`.
- **Minor:** `src/components/rename-modal.tsx:21,72`. Save is disabled while the field is empty, so the "Name can't be empty." error can only appear through keyboard submit. A sighted user sees a dead faint "Save" with no reason. Either keep Save enabled and show the error on press, or add the reason as a helper.
- **Minor / forward risk:** the frame path convention `frames/NNNNNN.jpg` is duplicated in `src/components/library-row.tsx:98` and `src/app/capture/[id].tsx:58`. The native capture plan in CLAUDE.md writes `images/Image_000001.jpg`. When Phase 7/8 lands, Library thumbnails and the Review hero/strip fall back silently to the cube or a blank. Extract one `frameUri(scan, i)` helper in `src/capture/` now.
