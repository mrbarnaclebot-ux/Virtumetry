Vertical slice 4 (the HUD's brain): the pure logic that turns capture events into "how ready is this scan, what does it cost, where have I looked, and would it pass checks" — the same answers on the scan screen, the review screen and the quote screen.

Output: `src/capture/{types,rules,capture-config,cost,readiness,coverage,precheck}.ts` + four test files.
</objective>
