Purpose: CAP-01 prep + priming, CAP-05/06/07 live HUD, CAP-10 fixture source in the real flow, CAP-09 save on stop.
Output: capture stack, prep + scan screens, HUD components, Library entry points, RNTL tests.
</objective>
