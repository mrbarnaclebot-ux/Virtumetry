## Phase Goal

**As a** the owner, **I want to** have an approved scan processed by real Echo-3 exactly once, inside my budget, and retry a retryable failure only after re-confirming the cost, **so that** a paid run never double-charges and never wastes money on a bad capture.

<objective>
Vertical slice 1 (the live client exists and is provably hermetic): the backend gains `echo3.ts` behind `ReconProvider`, a boot guard that needs two flags plus both secrets plus an https base, a fake Echo-3 HTTP server, and a vitest-wide guard that makes any call to SpAItial impossible from tests.

Purpose: PROC-05's live half needs a real client; the money rule needs mechanical enforcement, not discipline.
Output: echo3.ts, fake-echo3.ts, setup guard, config additions (`SPAITIAL_BASE_URL`, `BUDGET_ALERT_SHARE`), createProvider live branch, client contract tests.
</objective>
